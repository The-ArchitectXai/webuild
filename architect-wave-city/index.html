What our architectural design service includes

Good design for a plot in Wave City starts with the plot itself: its orientation, setbacks and the buildable envelope your township allows. Our architectural service covers the full set of documents your project needs before construction begins:

  • Site analysis and layout options for your plot
  • 3D renders and a short virtual walkthrough, so you see the home before it is built
  • Structural drawings and MEP (electrical, plumbing and services) drawings
  • BOQ, DPR and cost estimation, with area-wise cost details and complete material specifications
  • Support with map submission and authority approvals, as agreed in writing

We design residential, commercial and industrial spaces to industry standards, and every service we offer is checked against more than 100 checklist points.

Why design and construction under one roof works better

When the architect and the builder are separate firms, drawings often have to be reworked once real site and cost questions appear. At Webuild the same team that draws your plan also prepares the BOQ and runs the site. That means cost is visible from the first layout, material specifications are fixed before work starts, and nobody has to coordinate between separate designers, contractors and vendors on your behalf.

Start with the Map + Design Kit

Most Wave City owners begin with our Map + Design Kit, which turns your plot size into a concrete plan. We work with the plot sizes common in the township, from 135 sq yd up to 485 sq yd. The kit includes:

  • Site analysis: orientation, setbacks and buildable envelope
  • Two to three layout options on our base plans
  • 3D renders and a short walkthrough
  • A cost estimate by finish tier, with a timeline
  • Map-submission support, as agreed in writing

The kit is typically priced at ₹30 to ₹50 per sq ft and is fully credited against your construction contract if you sign within 60 days. A typical sequence is a free 30-minute consultation on day 0, agreement and token payment on days 1 to 2, a site visit and measurements on days 3 to 4, and a design presentation with walkthrough on days 10 to 12. You can get a rough number first with our Plan my home estimator.

Township rules and approvals

Floors, FAR, coverage and approval requirements differ by township and by plot, and they change what you can build. We check them before we draw, not after. Final approval always rests with the competent authority, so we plan the submission with you and keep you informed at each step instead of promising dates we do not control.

Frequently asked questions

What drawings will I receive?

Layout options, 3D renders and a walkthrough in the Map + Design Kit, followed by structural and MEP drawings, BOQ, DPR and estimation for the project you choose to build.

Do you handle map approval?

We support map submission as agreed in writing. The final decision rests with the authority, and timelines depend on its process.

How much does the design stage cost?

The Map + Design Kit typically costs ₹30 to ₹50 per sq ft. It is fully credited against the construction contract if you sign within 60 days.

How long does design take?

In our standard sequence the design presentation is ready 10 to 12 days after the kit is agreed. Complex plots or changes can take longer.
