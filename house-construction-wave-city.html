Our 6-step house construction process

  1. 1

    Client meeting and site assessment

    We understand your vision, budget and timeline, visit your plot, review site conditions and answer your questions.

  2. 2

    Onboarding and formal agreement

    You receive a detailed agreement with area-wise costing, complete material specifications and a milestone-based payment schedule. No hidden costs.

  3. 3

    Architectural design and authority approvals

    Architectural and structural drawings, 3D renderings and a BOQ. We support plot and building approvals as agreed in writing, and you walk through the design virtually before we break ground.

  4. 4

    Our site team runs foundation, structure, MEP and all trades. You can track progress with our project management tools, and our quality team inspects the work at each payment stage.

  5. 5

  6. 6

    Site completion and handover

    A final walkthrough, documentation and keys. We stand behind our work with a 45-day defect liability period.

Timeline and payment schedule

A typical new house takes 6 to 8 months from foundation to handover. The exact time depends on house size, site conditions, design complexity and weather. We set out the programme at the start and tell you early if anything threatens it.

Payment follows progress, so you pay as work gets done:

  • 1% token advance to book the project
  • 15% at foundation
  • For each floor: 20% when the slab is cast and 10% when brickwork is done (as a share of that floor's cost)
  • 10% after electrical work
  • 10% after plumbing work
  • 10% when interiors begin
  • The balance on completion, with 2% held for the 45-day defect liability period

Our quality team inspects the work at each stage before payment falls due.

Building in Wave City: what to know

Plots in Wave City commonly range from about 135 to 485 sq yd, which is roughly 1,200 to 4,400 sq ft. Rules on coverage, floors and setbacks differ by sector and by plot, so we check yours before we design, instead of assuming.

Final approvals rest with the competent authority, and timing depends on its process and on complete documentation. Our office is in Sector 2, Wave City, so site visits and on-site decisions are quick. Keep your plot documents ready for the first meeting.

What will it cost?

Cost depends on plot size, design, material quality and finish level. We offer three levels, Essential (standard, fastest), Signature (custom design) and Bespoke (premium, fully custom), and you can refine the choice during design.

For a rough estimate, answer a few questions in Plan my home. We confirm the exact cost, area by area, once we have reviewed your requirements.
