Our 6-Step House Construction Process

We've refined our approach over hundreds of projects. Here's exactly how we turn your vision into a finished home:

Step 1: Client Meeting & Site Assessment

We start with you. We understand your vision, budget, timeline, and unique needs. Our team visits your plot, assesses soil conditions, understands local Wave City regulations, and answers all your questions. This is where trust begins.

Step 2: Onboarding & Formal Agreement

You'll receive a detailed agreement with transparent area-wise costing, complete material specifications, and a milestone-based payment schedule. No hidden costs. No surprises.

Step 3: Architectural Design & Authority Approvals

Our architects create detailed architectural and structural drawings, 3D renderings, and BOQ (Bill of Quantities). We handle all interactions with Wave City authorities for plot approval, building permits, and compliance checks. You review via VR walkthroughs before we break ground.

Step 4: Project Execution & On-Site Management

Our site team takes over. We manage foundation work, structural construction, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) installation, and all trades. You track progress in real-time through our project management portal. At each milestone (foundation, slab, brickwork, electrical, plumbing), our team conducts extensive quality checks before you release payment.

Step 5: Interior Finishing & Furnishing

Once the shell is complete, we coordinate final finishes: flooring, wall finishes, false ceilings, doors, windows, and built-in storage. If you want furniture or decor, our interior design team can source and install it all. Your home becomes exactly as you imagined.

Step 6: Site Completion & Handover

We conduct a final walkthrough with you, address any last-minute questions, provide documentation, and hand you the keys. We stand behind our work with a 45-day defect liability period. If any issue surfaces, we fix it.

Timeline & Payment Schedule

Construction Timeline

For a typical new house in Wave City: 6–8 months from foundation to handover. The exact timeline depends on house size, soil conditions, complexity of design, and weather. We communicate deadlines upfront and manage risks proactively.

How You Pay

Payment is tied to progress, so you only pay as work gets done:

• 1% token advance upfront to book the project
• 15% at foundation stage
• 20% when each floor's slab is cast
• 10% when each floor's brickwork is complete
• 10% after electrical work
• 10% after plumbing work
• 10% when interiors begin
• Balance + final 2% (held for 45-day defect liability) on completion

At each payment stage, our quality team inspects the work. You have the right to approve (or request rectification) before releasing funds. Transparency built in.

Building in Wave City, Ghaziabad: What You Need to Know

Wave City is a well-planned township in Ghaziabad with established infrastructure. Here's what to expect for house construction:

  • Plot Sizes: Residential plots typically range from 250 sq. ft. to 2,000+ sq. ft.
  • Building Coverage: Usually 40–50% of plot area is allowed for construction.
  • Floor Height Limits: Most residential zones allow G+2 or G+3 depending on sector rules.
  • Approval Process: Wave City has a streamlined approval system. Most projects get authority clearance within 2–4 weeks with proper documentation.
  • Infrastructure: Paved roads, municipal water supply, reliable electricity, and good drainage already in place.
  • Community: Established schools, healthcare, shopping, parks—mature residential area with growing commercial activity.

Our team navigates Wave City's regulations and liaison process every month. We know the authorities, understand the requirements, and expedite approvals so your project starts on time.
