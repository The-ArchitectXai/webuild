Questions owners ask

How will I know what it costs?

You get area-wise cost details for the whole project, along with complete material specifications, so every rupee is accounted for.

What do you handle?

Architecture consulting, interior design, construction and interior furnishing under one roof, with no separate vendors to coordinate.

How do you keep quality high?

We check more than 100 checklist points across all levels of our services.

How long will my project take?

As a guide: a new home takes 6–8 months, a renovation 3–4 months, and a shop or office fit-out 45–60 days. The exact time depends on size and scope.

When do I pay?

Payment follows progress on site. You start with a 1% token advance. After that, payments fall due at set stages: 15% at foundation; for each floor, 20% when its slab is cast and 10% when its brickwork is done (as a share of that floor's cost); 10% after electrical work; 10% after plumbing; and 10% when interiors begin. The balance is paid on completion, with 2% held as security for the 45-day defect liability period. Percentages are of the contract value, except slab and brickwork, which are of each floor's cost.
