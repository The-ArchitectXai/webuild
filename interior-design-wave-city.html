Interiors that are beautiful and livable

At Webuild we create interiors that look good and work every day. From concept and material selection to final installation, we handle every detail. Whether you are designing a new home in Wave City, renovating an existing one or refreshing a single room, our interior design team works with you to create spaces that last.

Our interior design process

  1. 1

    Consultation and understanding

    We start by listening: your style, lifestyle and budget, and any inspiration you have. We visit your home to understand the layout, light and existing elements.

  2. 2

    Concept and design

    Our designers prepare concepts with mood boards, colour palettes and layout plans. You see 3D renderings and a virtual walkthrough before any work begins, and we refine the design with your feedback.

  3. 3

    Material selection and sourcing

    We select flooring, wall finishes, paint, tiles, furniture and lighting that match the design and your budget, and we manage suppliers and lead times.

  4. 4

    Execution and installation

    We coordinate and oversee carpenters, painters, electricians and contractors, manage the timeline and check quality at every stage.

  5. 5

    Final styling and handover

    Soft furnishings, decor and lighting bring the space to life. Then a final walkthrough, maintenance guidance and handover.

Timeline and budget

As a guide, the design phase (concept to approval) takes 2 to 4 weeks, sourcing and procurement 2 to 6 weeks depending on custom items, and execution and installation 2 to 8 weeks depending on scope. A single room usually takes 4 to 8 weeks in total, and a full home 2 to 4 months. We confirm a detailed timeline once we understand your project.

Budgets vary widely. As a guide, a room refresh may cost ₹2 to 5 lakh, and a full home ₹15 to 50+ lakh depending on scope and finishes. We design within your budget and give you a transparent breakdown of materials, labour, design fee and contingency.

Frequently asked questions

How do you design if I don't have a clear style in mind?

That is what we are here for. We ask about your lifestyle, the colours you are drawn to and what makes you comfortable, then show you several concepts. Design is a conversation.

Can you design around my existing furniture?

Yes. We design around pieces you want to keep, and suggest what to upgrade and what new items to add.

What if I love the design but dislike something after installation?

Small changes such as paint colour, accessories or furniture arrangement are easy and included in our scope. Larger changes carry additional cost, and we work with you to make it right.

How do you stay on budget?

We agree a clear budget upfront and design within it. We put money into the high-impact items first, such as flooring, wall colour and key pieces, and give you options at every price point.

Can I get design only and execute it myself?

Yes, we offer design-only packages with detailed drawings and specifications. Most clients choose our full service, because we handle sourcing, quality and installation.
