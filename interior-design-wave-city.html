Our Interior Design Process

We follow a structured approach to turn your vision into reality:

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Understanding

We start by listening. What's your style? What's your lifestyle? What's your budget? Do you have before-and-afters or inspiration images? We visit your home, understand the layout, light, and existing elements. No judgment—just understanding.

Step 2: Concept & Design

Our designers create multiple design concepts with mood boards, color palettes, and layout plans. You'll see 3D renderings and VR walkthroughs so you can experience the space before any work begins. We refine based on your feedback until it's perfect.

Step 3: Material Selection & Sourcing

We handle the overwhelming part: sourcing. Flooring, wall finishes, paint, tiles, furniture, lighting, decor—we select materials that match your design, fit your budget, and meet quality standards. We negotiate with suppliers and manage lead times.

Step 4: Execution & Installation

Our team coordinates and oversees installation: carpenters, painters, electricians, and contractors. We manage the timeline, coordinate trades, and ensure quality at every stage. You're kept updated throughout.

Step 5: Final Styling & Handover

Finishing touches matter. We add soft furnishings, decor, plants, and lighting to bring the space to life. Final walk-through, training on maintenance, and you're done. Your new space is ready.

Timeline & Budget

How Long Does Interior Design Take?

📅 Design phase (concept to approval): 2–4 weeks
📅 Sourcing and procurement: 2–6 weeks (depending on custom items)
📅 Execution and installation: 2–8 weeks (depends on scope)

A single room? 4–8 weeks total. A full home? 2–4 months. We provide a detailed timeline after understanding your project scope.

How Much Does It Cost?

Interior design budgets vary widely. A room refresh might be ₹2–5 lakh. A full home could be ₹15–50+ lakh depending on scope and finishes. We work within your budget and help you allocate resources wisely.

We provide transparent cost breakdowns: materials, labor, design fee, contingency. You know what every rupee is going toward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do you design if I don't have a clear style in mind?
A: That's what we're here for. We ask about your lifestyle, colors you're drawn to, inspiration images, and what makes you comfortable. We show you multiple concepts and help you discover what resonates. Design is a conversation.
Q: Can you design around my existing furniture?
A: Absolutely. If you have pieces you love and want to keep, we design around them. We can also suggest which pieces to upgrade and source new items that complement your existing ones.
Q: What if I love the design but hate something after installation?
A: Small changes—paint color, accessories, furniture rearrangement—are easy and included in our scope. Larger changes have additional costs, but we work with you to make it right.
Q: How do you stay on budget?
A: We set a clear budget upfront and design within it. We prioritize high-impact items (flooring, wall color, key pieces) and allocate remaining budget wisely. We give you options at every price point.
Q: Can I do just the design and execute it myself?
A: Yes, we offer design-only packages with detailed drawings and specifications. But we've found that most clients prefer our full service—we handle sourcing, quality, installation, and stress. It's worth it.
